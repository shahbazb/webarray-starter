The market writes the menu

We cook over a single oak fire. Every morning the market decides the menu: the fish that came in, the vegetables at their best, the herbs from our own planters.

Dough is mixed at nine, the fire is lit at two, and by five the room smells of smoke, citrus and anise.

  1. 09:00Dough is mixed
  2. 14:00The fire is lit
  3. 17:00Smoke, citrus and anise

Chef Daria Kostas

Chef Daria Kostas grew up between her grandmother's island kitchen and the city's busiest grills.

At Ember & Anise she cooks the food she likes to eat: generous, a little smoky, and built for sharing.
